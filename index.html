How a recovery actually happens

An item can find its way back to its owner through three different paths — all of them end at the same verification step.

Someone reports it found

A student who found an item lists it, with where and when it turned up.

“I found this”

A finder can respond directly to an existing lost report instead of creating a new one.

Handed to the desk

Physically dropped-off items are logged by campus staff and matched the same way.
